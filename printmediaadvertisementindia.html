And that newspaper ads have 77% higher recall rates than online ads? That’s why businesses continue to invest in print advertising for stronger brand visibility and credibility.

At Buttercup Advertising Studio, we help businesses create high-impact print ads that grab attention, engage customers, and drive sales. Whether you need newspaper ads, magazine ads, brochures, flyers, or billboards, our team of experts delivers strategic, creative, and result-oriented print media campaigns for brands across India.

Why Print Media Advertising is Still Important?

  • Higher Brand Credibility – People trust print ads more because they feel authentic and reliable compared to online pop-ups.
  • Better Attention & Retention – Unlike digital ads, which can be skipped in seconds, print ads stay longer and have a lasting impact.
  • Local & National Reach – Newspaper and magazine ads help brands target specific cities, states, or national audiences effectively.
  • Longer Shelf Life – A digital ad disappears quickly, but a newspaper, magazine, or brochure stays with customers for weeks or months.
  • Stronger Engagement – Studies show that print media has a 70% higher engagement rate than digital ads.
