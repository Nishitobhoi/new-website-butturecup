Stainless Steel Coils

Metronox offers premium-quality cold-rolled stainless steel coils manufactured with precise thickness control and superior surface finish. Our coils are produced using advanced cold-rolling and controlled annealing processes to achieve excellent mechanical properties, dimensional accuracy, and consistent quality.

These stainless steel coils are widely used in fabrication, construction, automotive components, kitchenware, home appliances, and various industrial applications.

Manufacturing Process

  • Cold Rolling: Performed to achieve accurate thickness and enhanced mechanical strength
  • Annealing: Restores ductility and relieves internal stresses developed during rolling
  • Precision Coiling: Ensures safe handling, efficient storage, and convenient transportation

Grades Available

201, 202, N1, N7, 304, 304L, 316, 316L

Specifications

  • Thickness: 0.08 mm and above
  • Width: 18 mm to 830 mm
  • Tolerance: ±0.005 mm
  • Form: Coil, Circles & Sheets
  • Surface Finish: 2B, BA, No.4, Bright Annealed
  • Edge Condition: Mill Edge, Slit Edge, Deburred Edge, Rounded Edge
  • Temper: Annealed, ¼ Hard, ½ Hard, ¾ Hard, Full Hard, Extra Hard
  • Packaging: Securely packed using industry-standard protective materials to prevent surface and edge damage during handling, storage, and transportation.

Standards

BIS,ASTM, ASME, EN, JIS, and other international standards

Our advanced machinery and expert craftsmanship enable professional stainless steel cutting and polishing, delivering accurate dimensions and high-quality surface finishes suitable for architectural, industrial, Houseware, and decorative applications.

Our Services

  • Stainless Steel Slitting – Precision cutting of coils into narrower widths to meet your exact requirements.

Stainless Steel Slitting Services

Stainless Steel Slitting is the precision process of cutting wide stainless steel coils into narrower widths, creating slit coils as per customer specifications. At Metro Bright Bar (India) Pvt. Ltd., our advanced slitting facilities ensure every slit coil is produced with excellent edge quality, dimensional accuracy, and minimal waste.

Key Features

  • High-Precision Slitter Heads – For consistent, accurate cutting.
  • Automated Tension Control Systems – Ensures uniform slit coil quality.
  • Quick Tooling Change – Increases productivity and reduces downtime.
  • Accurate Width Control & Burr-Free Edges – Perfectly finished slit coils every time.
  • Compatible with Various Stainless Steel Grades & Thicknesses – Flexible solutions for diverse needs.

Our stainless steel slitting services optimize material usage, reduce waste, and enhance processing efficiency, delivering high-quality slit coils ready for further fabrication or industrial applications.

Stainless Steel Cut-to-Length Sheets

Our Cut-to-Length (CTL) services cut stainless steel coils into flat sheets of exact required length. These sheets are delivered flat-stacked and stress-free, making them ideal for further fabrication processes such as laser cutting, bending, blanks, and welding.

Specifications

  • Thickness: 0.10 mm to 4.00 mm
  • Width: 100 mm to 1250 mm
  • Length: 100 mm to 6000 mm

Advantages

  • Dead-flat sheets
  • High dimensional accuracy
  • Reduced material loss
  • Suitable for precision fabrication

Stainless Steel Coil Polishing

Our Coil Polishing service enhances the surface quality of stainless steel coils, including BA coils, while maintaining uniform thickness and finish across the entire length. This process is ideal for applications requiring consistent appearance, superior aesthetics, and premium surface quality.

Benefits

  • Uniform Surface Finish – Consistency across the entire coil length.
  • Enhanced Corrosion Resistance – Protects the metal for longer life.
  • Improved Visual Appeal – Perfect for decorative and architectural uses.
  • Suitable for Decorative & Architectural Applications – Meets high-quality design standards.
  • PVC Lamination Friendly – Ideal for subsequent PVC coating or lamination processes.

Our coil polishing service ensures that every BA coil is polished to perfection, ready for industrial, architectural, or decorative applications.
