who we are

Where Stainless Steel Meets Precision, Agility, and Trust

Metronox Pvt. Ltd. stands as a future-focused leader in India’s Stainless Steel ecosystem, empowering industries with high-performance SS Coils, Pipes, and Sheets. With a strong footprint across critical sectors: Utensils, Automotive, and Steel Furniture, Metronox is trusted for materials that elevate both product quality and production efficiency.

Founded in 2016 as Metro Bright Bar (India) Pvt. Ltd., the company has grown with a clear vision: to become India’s most agile, dependable, and quality-driven stainless steel partner. Every decision, investment, and process at Metronox reflects this singular commitment.
