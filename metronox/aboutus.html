Our Journey:
Built on Insight, Strengthened by Innovation

Our founders, armed with decades of experience in the steel business, observed a persistent challenge across manufacturing industries, long lead times, unpredictable supply, and the compulsion to maintain large inventories. This inspired the creation of a dedicated Stainless Steel Service Centre, fundamentally transforming the customer experience from conventional bulk selling to a responsive, just-in-time supply model.

This shift empowered manufacturers of Utensils, Pipes, Automotive components, and related products to dramatically reduce stockholding, accelerate production cycles, and maintain uninterrupted operations.

The Next Leap: Integrated Excellence

In 2024, with the commissioning of our state-of-the-art Cold Rolling Facility, we reintroduced ourselves as Metronox Pvt. Ltd. a fully integrated entity with control over every stage of the supply chain. This advancement ensures unmatched product consistency, enhanced surface quality, and reliable uniformity across every SS Coil, Pipe, and Sheet we supply.

Today, Metronox stands equipped not only with manufacturing capability but also with the agility and intelligence to support modern industries with the precision and predictability they demand.
